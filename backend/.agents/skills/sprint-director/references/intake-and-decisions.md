# Intake and decisions

## Intake

1. List every file and link.
2. Assign stable `SRC-NNN` IDs.
3. Sample enough to identify type, scope, relevance, confidentiality, missing
   access, and duplicates.
4. Separate organizer facts from team assumptions.
5. Preserve raw sources and propagate IDs to derived artifacts.

During initial configuration, avoid full cleaning/indexing. Do only what is
needed to select the product and architecture; independent preparation may
follow after work division.

## Five-question round

1. Who has the painful decision/action, and what does it cost now?
2. What exact input can the demo reliably receive?
3. What grounded recommendation/action proves value in under two minutes?
4. What would an obvious document chatbot do, and how is this different?
5. What sources, APIs, data, credentials, and skills are available now?

Use at most three conditional follow-ups for dangerous errors, evidence shape,
the single differentiator, ownership, or kill conditions.

## Option pack

For each consequential decision provide:

- failure/capability addressed and available evidence;
- A: lowest-risk baseline;
- B: best expected score, normally recommended;
- C: higher upside;
- creative alternatives and why they differ;
- pros, cons, effort, weighted comparison;
- recommendation, deadline, and kill/revisit condition.

Score user value, innovation, trust, demo clarity, data readiness, integration
time, and failure risk. Record the final choice and rejected alternatives in
the decision log.
